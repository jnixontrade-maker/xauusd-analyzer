BSL Sweep → Short ~68% WR
1. Identify swing high (Buy Side Liquidity) on 4H or 1H
2. Watch for wick above BSL — the stop hunt
3. Wait for CHoCH — first bearish candle closing below prior swing low
4. Mark FVG created during the sweep impulse
5. Wait for price to retrace INTO FVG (IFVG) — entry trigger
6. Enter SHORT. SL above BSL high. TP = previous SSL
💡 Best on 4H sweeps. Enter on 15M/1M. London & NY sessions only.
SSL Sweep → Long ~65% WR
1. Identify swing low (Sell Side Liquidity) on 1H or 4H
2. Watch for wick below SSL — stop hunt of longs
3. Wait for CHoCH: first bullish close above previous swing high
4. Mark FVG created in the bullish expansion impulse
5. Wait for pullback into FVG — expect bounce
6. Enter LONG. SL below SSL low. TP = next BSL above
💡 Works best when Daily bias is bullish.
FVG / IFVG Precision Entry ~72% WR
1. FVG = 3 candles where candle 1 high and candle 3 low have a gap
2. Mark the FVG zone. Bullish: c1.high → c3.low. Bearish: c1.low → c3.high
3. IFVG = price RETURNS into the FVG zone — actual entry
4. Wait for rejection wick forming inside FVG on 1M or 5M
5. Combine with 15M+ BOS in direction + OB overlapping FVG
6. SL just outside FVG. TP = next liquidity level or 2:1 minimum
💡 FVG + Order Block overlap = premium zone. NEVER enter FVG against 4H trend.
Expansion Model — Trader Zed ~70% WR
1. LIQUIDITY GRAB — price sweeps clear BSL or SSL (permission to trade)
2. STRUCTURE SHIFT (CHoCH) — first opposing candle breaks a recent swing
3. FVG AT SHIFT — impulse creates FVG, price returns = entry
Same Model. Same Time. Same Results. Every day on Gold.
💡 Best at London open 08:00 UTC, NY open 13:30 UTC. 4H timeframe.
No-Wick Candle Entry — Bard.fx ~85% WR
1. Identify key level — supply/demand zone, FVG, or liquidity level
2. Mark it up. Wait for price to retrace back to that area
3. BULLISH entry: wait for candle with NO BOTTOM WICK closing into zone
4. BEARISH entry: wait for candle with NO TOP WICK — pure momentum
5. Enter on the close of the no-wick candle
6. SL below/above the zone. TP = next liquidity level
💡 No-wick = pure momentum, no rejection. Highest WR of all strategies.
ORB — Opening Range Breakout ~67% WR
1. Mark the opening range — high and low of first 5-15 mins after session open
2. Wait for price to BREAK above the high or below the low on volume
3. Wait for RETEST of the broken level (old resistance becomes support)
4. Enter on rejection candle at the retest
5. SL below opposite end of the range. TP = measured move
💡 London open 08:00 UTC and NY open 13:30 UTC only. Ignore Asian session ORB.
