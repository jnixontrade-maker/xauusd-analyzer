Expansion Model
BSL Sweep → Short ~68% WR
1. Identify swing high (Buy Side Liquidity) on 4H or 1H
2. Watch for wick or candle close above BSL — the stop hunt
3. Wait for CHoCH — first bearish candle closing below a prior swing low
4. Mark FVG created during the sweep impulse
5. Wait for price to retrace INTO FVG zone (IFVG) — your entry trigger
6. Enter SHORT on rejection wick. SL above BSL high. TP = previous SSL
💡 4H for clean sweeps. 15M/1M for entry timing. London & NY sessions only.
SSL Sweep → Long ~65% WR
1. Identify swing low (Sell Side Liquidity) on 1H or 4H
2. Watch for wick below SSL — stop hunt of longs
3. Wait for CHoCH: first bullish close above previous swing high
4. Mark FVG created in the bullish expansion impulse
5. Wait for pullback into FVG (IFVG) — expect bounce
6. Enter LONG on rejection. SL below SSL low. TP = next BSL above
💡 Works best when Daily bias is bullish. Check 4H for higher BSL target.
FVG / IFVG Precision Entry ~72% WR
1. FVG = 3 candles where candle 1 high and candle 3 low have a gap
2. Mark the FVG zone. Bullish: c1.high → c3.low. Bearish: c1.low → c3.high
3. IFVG = price RETURNS into the FVG zone — this is the actual entry
4. Wait for rejection wick forming inside FVG on 1M or 5M
5. Combine with: 15M+ BOS in direction + OB overlapping FVG
6. SL just outside the FVG. TP = next liquidity level or 2:1 minimum
💡 FVG + Order Block overlap = premium zone. NEVER enter FVG against 4H trend.
Expansion Model (3 Criteria) ~70% WR
1. LIQUIDITY GRAB — price sweeps a clear BSL or SSL (your permission to trade)
2. STRUCTURE SHIFT (CHoCH) — first opposing candle breaks a recent swing
3. FVG/IFVG AT SHIFT — the shift impulse creates a FVG, price returns = entry
Same Model. Same Time. Same Results. The model repeats because liquidity is always swept before expansion.
